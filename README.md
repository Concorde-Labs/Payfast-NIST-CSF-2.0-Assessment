# Payfast Inc. | NIST CSF 2.0 Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Project Overview

Payfast Inc. is a fintech organization providing digital payment services to individuals and small businesses. With approximately 180 employees and operations supported by cloud applications, internal systems, and third-party integrations, maintaining effective cybersecurity governance is essential to protecting sensitive information and ensuring operational resilience.

An assessment of Payfast's cybersecurity environment identified weaknesses in asset management, access controls, security monitoring, incident response, and third-party risk management.

Using the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**, the assessment examines the organization's current security posture against desired cybersecurity outcomes, identifies control gaps, and establishes a risk-informed remediation roadmap. Relevant **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5** control families support the recommended improvements.

## Assessment Objectives

- Map five core business processes to relevant NIST CSF 2.0 functions.
- Evaluate existing cybersecurity practices and define desired security outcomes.
- Identify gaps between Current and Target Profiles.
- Map identified gaps to applicable NIST SP 800-53 control families.
- Develop a phased implementation plan with proposed responsibilities, remediation actions, and verification requirements.

## Assessment Scope

The assessment focuses on five business processes:

1. Customer Onboarding & Account Management
2. Payment Processing
3. Asset & System Management
4. Employee Access Management
5. Third-Party Payment Integrations

Relevant cybersecurity weaknesses include asset visibility, security awareness, access governance, authentication, security monitoring, incident response, third-party risk management, and recovery preparedness.

## Frameworks Applied

**Primary Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

**Supporting Control Catalogue:** NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5

**NIST CSF Functions:** Govern (GV), Identify (ID), Protect (PR), Detect (DE), Respond (RS), and Recover (RC).
